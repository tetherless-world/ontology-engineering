## Terminology List

**Current version: OE Week 8.**

[Download the current curated term list (.xlsx)](files/term-lists/OE_8_TeamCJF_CuratedTermList.xlsx)

The workbook documents the project's conversation move classes, relationships, states, provenance concepts, and state assignment vocabulary. The `PrioritizedTerms` worksheet records preferred labels, definitions, sources, scope and usage notes, examples, dependencies, and team contributions. The `Sources` worksheet contains the supporting references.

The current vocabulary uses **conversation move** for the persistent contribution represented in the ontology. Its main move types are Information Request, Issue, Option, Claim, Expressed Position, and Outcome. State Assignment records connect moves with states, effective utterances, evidence, and preceding assignments. See the [conceptual model and executable ontology](ontology.html) for the class hierarchy and property relationships.
