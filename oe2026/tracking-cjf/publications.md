## Publications

### An Ontology for Tracking Collective Judgment Formation in Multiparty Deliberation

Hernandez, Tiziana, Devin De Silva, Srihari Sridharan, and Matthew Lausev. "An Ontology for Tracking Collective Judgment Formation in Multiparty Deliberation." Report outline. RPI Technical Report, Ontology Engineering 2026. Troy, NY: Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, November 2026.

The paper introduces the Tracking CJF ontology for conversation moves, their evidence-bearing relationships, and their state histories. It evaluates the ontology with six competency questions over a 28-turn platform-selection discussion, and discusses the value of semantics, limitations, and the path to a multiparty conversational agent. This version is the OE Week 12 report outline; the draft and final papers will be added here when they are available.
