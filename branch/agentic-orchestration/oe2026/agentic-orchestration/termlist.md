## Terminology List

**Current version: 11 (Assignment 12, November 20, 2026).** Every class and property of the [ontology](ontology.html)
has a row with the same label and definition; the individuals are not listed. New in this version: a row for has step
position (hasStepPosition), the position of a step in its plan's execution order. Many other rows have fuller references
and rewritten contexts, notes and examples that describe agent orchestration in general rather than pointing back to the
use case. The [redlined copy](files/terminology/OE_11_TeamAgenticCapabilities_RedlinedTermList.xlsx) highlights the new
row in yellow and the changed cells in green.
