The workbook documents the project's conversation move classes, relationships, states, provenance concepts, and state assignment vocabulary. The `PrioritizedTerms` worksheet records preferred labels, alternate labels and synonyms, identifiers, definitions and their sources, scope, explanatory and usage notes, examples, dependencies, parent and equivalent concepts, references, and the team members who introduced and completed each term.

The workbook uses **conversation move** for the persistent contribution represented in the ontology. Its main move types are Information Request, Issue, Option, Claim, Expressed Position, and Outcome. State Assignment records connect moves with states, effective utterances, evidence, and preceding assignments. See the [conceptual model and executable ontology](ontology.html) for the class hierarchy and property relationships.

The Week 13 workbook lists 92 terms, including relationship classes such as Refinement, Select, and About and the properties that connect reified relationships to their source and target moves.
